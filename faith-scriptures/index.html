Daily Devotion

Portals of Prayer

"Portals of Prayer" is our recommended daily devotional guide, published by Concordia Publishing House. Each day features a scripture reading, thoughtful meditation, and prayer to help you connect with God throughout your day.

Today's Devotional Theme

"Walking With God"

"He has shown you, O mortal, what is good. And what does the Lord require of you? To act justly and to love mercy and to walk humbly with your God."

Micah 6:8

Walking with God means staying close to Him in daily life: trusting His pace, listening for His Word, praying through worries, and noticing His presence in ordinary moments. It is not about perfection; it is about living in relationship with the One who loves us most.

Copies of Portals of Prayer are available at the church entrance, or you can subscribe online for daily email delivery.

The Lutheran Hour

The Lutheran Hour is the world's longest-running Christian outreach radio program, airing weekly messages of hope and salvation in Jesus Christ. Produced by Lutheran Hour Ministries, each episode features a sermon from a Lutheran pastor on topics relevant to daily life and faith.

You can listen to The Lutheran Hour on Sunday mornings by radio, throughout the day in the Windsor area, or stream episodes anytime online. It is a wonderful way to stay connected to God's Word during the week.

CHOK 1070 from Sarnia

Broadcast Sundays at 8:30 AM.

WRDT 560 AM, Windsor Area

Broadcast Sundays at 7:30 AM, 12:00 PM, and 6:30 PM.

Online Streaming

Listen anytime at lhm.org or through the Lutheran Hour Canada radio player at LLL.ca.
