About Our Church

Serving Kingsville Since 1947

First Lutheran Church has been a beacon of faith in the Kingsville community for over 75 years. Founded by a small group of dedicated families, our church has grown into a vibrant congregation that welcomes people from all walks of life.

We are a congregation of Lutheran Church-Canada, committed to sharing the Gospel of Jesus Christ through worship, education, and service. Our mission is to make disciples of all nations, baptizing and teaching in the name of the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

Whether you are a lifelong Lutheran or exploring faith for the first time, you will find a warm welcome here. Join us for worship and discover the joy of belonging to a faith community.
