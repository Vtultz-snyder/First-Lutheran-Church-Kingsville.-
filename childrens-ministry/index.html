Children's Ministry

Nurturing faith in the next generation through Bible stories, crafts, and fun.

Sunday School

Sunday School Is on Pause This Fall

The start of fall Sunday School has been delayed while we seek direction for this ministry. Children are very welcome in worship, and for September you will find clipboard activities for them in the Narthex.

Jennifer Freeman and Pamela Bedard have stepped away from the Sunday School Ministry after three years of faithful service. We are grateful for all they did to give our youngest members a safe and caring place to learn about Jesus, and we ask for prayer for both families.

Our leadership team hopes to offer Sunday School in some form. If you feel called to lead, teach, or share ideas, please speak to Becky Bunn, or email sundayschoolfirstlutheran@gmail.com.

What We Do

Activities & Programs

Our children's program is designed to be fun, engaging, and spiritually nourishing.

Bible Stories

Children explore the stories of the Bible through age-appropriate lessons and engaging storytelling.

Arts & Crafts

Creative projects help children express their faith and remember the lessons they've learned.

Music & Songs

Singing is a joyful part of our program. Children learn hymns and praise songs together.

Friendship & Fun

Children build lasting friendships in a safe, nurturing environment with caring adult leaders.

Annual Events

Special Events for Kids

Christmas Pageant

Children reenact the nativity story with costumes, music, and joy for the congregation.

Vacation Bible School

A week-long summer program filled with Bible lessons, games, crafts, and snacks.

Easter Egg Hunt

An annual celebration of the resurrection with an egg hunt and fun activities.
